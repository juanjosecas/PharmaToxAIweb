OECD adopted TG 445A on 2 July 2026. It addresses induction of CYP1A2, CYP3A4 and CYP2B6 using differentiated human hepatic cells, with greater uncertainty for CYP2B6.

## References

OECD (2026). [TG 445A](https://doi.org/10.1787/765aaada-en).

## Context

The assay measures functional enzyme activity, not a clinical outcome.

CYP induction can change parent-compound and metabolite exposure. CYP1A2 is linked to AhR, CYP3A4 to PXR and CAR, and CYP2B6 particularly to CAR. The pathways overlap; a functional response does not demonstrate toxicity by itself.

## Method

The validated assay uses differentiated human HepaRG cells exposed for 48 hours, with induction medium renewed at approximately 24 hours. At least six concentrations are tested. After exposure, phenacetin, bupropion and midazolam serve as probe substrates for CYP1A2, CYP2B6 and CYP3A, respectively. The corresponding metabolites are quantified by LC–MS, and catalytic activity is normalised to protein content. The result is a functional enzyme-activity measurement, rather than a transcriptomic marker [1].
