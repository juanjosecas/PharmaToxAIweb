03 / Targeted experimental follow-up

Proposed validation plan: HepG2 and human dermal fibroblasts; orthogonal metabolic-activity (MTT) and ATP-based (CellTiter-Glo) viability readouts; exposure–response curves; intracellular DCFH-DA oxidation with interference controls; glutathione depletion where mechanistically relevant; exploratory microplate bacterial reverse-mutation screening in TA98/TA100 ±S9.

A two-strain microplate screen is not a full OECD TG 471 bacterial reverse mutation test. A fold-change is not sufficient for a positive Ames classification; controls, dose response, cytotoxicity, reproducibility, appropriate strain coverage and test-design-specific criteria are needed. DCFH-DA is not ROS-species-specific; MTT is a metabolic readout, not a direct cell count.

Methods / references

Framework and scope

ICH M7(R2), Assessment and Control of DNA Reactive (Mutagenic) Impurities in Pharmaceuticals: ICH guideline (PDF). Two complementary (Q)SAR methodologies and expert review support impurity assessment; classifications depend on context and evidence. Nitrosamines may require compound-specific approaches and additional regulatory guidance.

OECD Principles for the Validation of (Q)SAR Models: OECD guidance. Applicability domain must be defined for the specific model. Bacterial reverse mutation: OECD Test Guideline 471.

Read-across: EFSA guidance (2025; food/feed-specific scope). This reference informs scientific documentation and does not imply automatic acceptance under other jurisdictions.

Reporting levels: rule/ML outputs = predictions; microplate and cellular results = experimental observations only when actually measured; final risk decision = expert evidence integration. This page contains synthetic demonstrations at every level.
